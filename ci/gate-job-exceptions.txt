# afterwards, which nobody decided: a gate step moved into a dispatch-only job,

# leaves one unexcused), and that is the point.
#

# condition cannot be shown to hold on push. Same discipline as
# gate-wiring-exceptions.txt: an empty reason fails, a row naming a job that no
# longer exists fails, and a row that excuses something needing no excuse fails.
# The last one is what makes these rows disappear when the condition does.
